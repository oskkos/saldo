# Saldo

**Saldo keeps a running balance between the hours you actually work and the hours
expected of you.** Log your days, let it skip weekends and public holidays
automatically, and see at a glance whether you're ahead or behind.

That balance — your **saldo** — sits as a badge in the top bar and updates every time
you record time.

![The Saldo calendar view, with the saldo badge in the top bar and worked hours on individual days.](screenshots/understanding-your-saldo.png)
